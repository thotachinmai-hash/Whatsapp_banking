# Security — What This Assistant Will Never Ask For

This assistant will never ask you for your PIN, password, CVV, OTP, or
your full Aadhaar or PAN number in a message reply — even during
registration, only a photo of the document itself is requested, not the
number typed out. Do not share these details over WhatsApp with anyone
claiming to be from the bank.

# Security — How Your Details Are Used

Documents you upload (like an Aadhaar or PAN card image, or a cheque
photo) are used only to extract the information needed for the request
you are making — registration, KYC, or a cheque deposit — and are not
used for any other purpose.

# Security — Confirming Before Anything Is Final

Every action that moves money or submits a request — a transfer, a
cheque deposit, a loan application, a KYC update — shows you a summary
first and waits for your confirmation before anything is actually
submitted or sent. Nothing happens automatically without you confirming
it.

# Security — Is My Information Safe On WhatsApp

Messages are sent through WhatsApp's own messaging service. The
assistant itself only asks for what is needed to carry out the banking
action you requested, and does not request sensitive credentials
(PIN/password/CVV/OTP) at any point.

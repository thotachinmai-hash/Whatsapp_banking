# Documents Required — Personal Loan

To apply for a personal loan you will need:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips (if salaried)
- Last 3 months' bank statements
- A recent passport-size photograph

No property or collateral documents are needed for a personal loan.

# Documents Required — Home Loan

To apply for a home loan you will need:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips (if salaried) or last 2 years' income tax returns (if self-employed)
- Last 6 months' bank statements
- Property documents: sale agreement or title deed, approved building plan
- A recent passport-size photograph

# Documents Required — Vehicle Loan

To apply for a vehicle loan you will need:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Last 3 months' salary slips or income proof
- Last 3 months' bank statements
- Vehicle quotation or proforma invoice from the dealer
- A recent passport-size photograph

# Documents Required — Education Loan

To apply for an education loan you will need:
- PAN card
- Aadhaar card
- Admission letter from the institution
- Fee structure from the institution
- Last 3 months' bank statements
- Co-applicant's (parent/guardian) income proof
- A recent passport-size photograph

# General Loan Eligibility Notes

Document checklists above are what is needed to apply — they are not a
guarantee of approval. Final eligibility depends on income, existing
obligations, and credit profile, and is decided during the actual
application review. This information is general guidance, not a decision
for any specific applicant.

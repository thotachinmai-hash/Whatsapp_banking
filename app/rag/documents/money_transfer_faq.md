# Money Transfer — How It Works

To send money, tell the assistant who you want to pay and how much — for
example "Transfer 500 to Priya" or "Send money to Rahul". If the person
is already a saved beneficiary (payee), the transfer goes straight to
choosing which of your accounts to send from. If they are new, you will
be asked for their name and account number once; they are then saved
automatically for next time, so you do not need to re-enter their
details for a future payment.

# Money Transfer — Confirmation Before Sending

Before any transfer is completed, you are shown a summary — beneficiary
name, account, and amount — and asked to confirm. Nothing is sent until
you reply to confirm; you can also edit the amount or cancel the
transfer at that point instead.

# Money Transfer — Is There A Transfer Limit Or Sending Fee

There is no separate fixed transfer limit — a transfer can be made for
any amount up to your available account balance. There is no fee
charged for sending a transfer through this assistant.

# Money Transfer — NEFT, RTGS, Or IMPS

This assistant does not ask you to choose a transfer method like NEFT,
RTGS, or IMPS — there is a single, direct transfer process. You simply
give the beneficiary and amount, and confirm; there is no separate mode
to select.

# Money Transfer — Do You Need An OTP Or PIN To Send Money

No. Confirming the on-screen summary is what authorises the transfer —
you are never asked for an OTP, PIN, password, or card details to
approve a payment through this assistant.

# Money Transfer — Choosing Which Account To Send From

If you have more than one account, you will be asked which one to send
the transfer from. If you only have a single account, it is used
automatically without asking.

# Money Transfer — Sent To The Wrong Account

Once a transfer is confirmed, it cannot be automatically reversed or
recalled through this assistant — there is no undo after confirmation.
This is exactly why the confirmation summary (beneficiary name, account,
and amount) is shown before anything is sent: review it carefully,
especially the account number, before confirming.

# Money Transfer — Cancelling Or Stopping Partway Through

You can say "Cancel" or "Stop" at any point while a transfer is in
progress. You will be asked to confirm you want to stop (so a message
like "cancel" typed by mistake does not immediately discard your
progress) — reply to confirm, and the transfer is discarded with
nothing sent and no in-progress details kept.

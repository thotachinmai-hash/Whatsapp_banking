# Cheque Deposit — How It Works

To deposit a cheque through this WhatsApp assistant, upload a clear photo
of the cheque. The photo should show the whole cheque — payee name,
amount in figures and words, date, and signature area — clearly and in
good lighting. This is also how you submit, drop off, or send in a
cheque — there is no separate physical drop-off needed.

# Cheque Deposit — Common Reasons A Cheque Is Rejected

A cheque submission may be sent back for correction (or rejected) if:
- The payee name does not match the registered account holder's name
- The amount in figures is missing or unreadable
- The image is blurry, cropped, or too dark to read
- A mandatory field (payee or amount) could not be read from the image

If your cheque request was rejected, check that the photo is clear and
that the payee name on the cheque matches your registered name, then
try uploading it again.

# Cheque Deposit — What The Status Values Mean

Every cheque deposit request has a status:
- **PENDING** — accepted and waiting to be processed
- **COMPLETED** — processed and cleared
- **REJECTED** — sent back; see the common rejection reasons above

# Cheque Deposit — How Long Clearing Takes

Once a cheque deposit request is accepted, it is marked PENDING and then
processed by the bank. Clearing times vary depending on the issuing bank
and cheque type; you can check the current status of a submitted cheque
at any time by asking for its status, for example "check my cheque
CHQ-XXXXXXXX" or "has my cheque been processed yet".

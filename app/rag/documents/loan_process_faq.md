# Loan Application — How The Process Works

To apply for a loan, tell the assistant which type you want (personal,
home, vehicle, or education) or just say "I want a loan" and you will
be shown the options. You will then be asked to provide the required
details and documents, shown a summary to confirm, and given a
trackable request ID once submitted. Nothing is submitted until you
confirm the summary.

# Loan Application — What Is EMI

EMI stands for "Equated Monthly Instalment" — the fixed monthly amount
you would repay on a loan, covering both interest and part of the
principal, over the loan's tenure (repayment period). The exact EMI for
a loan depends on the amount borrowed, the interest rate, and the
tenure chosen; ask about a specific loan type's rate and tenure range
for real figures.

# Loan Application — Checking Your Application Status

Once you have applied, you can check the status of your loan
application at any time by asking, for example "check my loan status"
or "what's the status of my loan application". If you do not provide a
request ID, all of your loan applications linked to your registered
number are shown.

# Loan Application — Can I Apply For More Than One Loan Type

Yes — there is no restriction on applying for a loan type based on any
other loan you may already have. Each application is reviewed and
tracked separately with its own request ID.

# Loan Application — Prepayment Or Paying Off Early

General loan practice allows a loan to be paid off before the end of
its tenure (prepayment or foreclosure), though specific terms and any
applicable charges depend on the loan type and are confirmed during the
application. This assistant does not currently process prepayment
requests directly — ask about a specific loan's terms for the most
accurate answer.

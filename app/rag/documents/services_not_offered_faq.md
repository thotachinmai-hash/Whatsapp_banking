# Services Not Offered — Physical Debit Or Credit Cards

This assistant does not currently issue physical or virtual debit or
credit cards. Banking is carried out entirely through this WhatsApp
assistant — checking balances, viewing transactions, transferring
money, depositing cheques, and applying for loans.

# Services Not Offered — ATM Or Branch Access

This assistant does not have a physical branch or ATM network — it is a
WhatsApp-only banking service. Cash cannot be withdrawn or deposited
through it.

# Services Not Offered — Overdraft Facility

There is no overdraft facility available through this assistant — a
transfer can only be made up to your available account balance.

# Services Not Offered — Fixed Deposits (FD)

This assistant does not currently support opening, renewing, or
withdrawing a fixed deposit (FD). Money can only be held in your
regular savings, current, or salary account through this assistant.

# Services Not Offered — Demand Drafts (DD)

This assistant does not currently support issuing a demand draft (DD).
Money can be sent to another person only via a transfer to a saved or
new beneficiary.

# Services Not Offered — Closing An Account

This assistant does not currently support closing an account through
WhatsApp. It can be used to check balances, view transactions, deposit
cheques, apply for a loan, update KYC, transfer money, and open an
additional account.

# What This Assistant Does Offer

Through this WhatsApp assistant you can: check your account balance,
view transactions and spend summaries by category, transfer money to a
saved or new beneficiary, deposit a cheque by photo, apply for a
personal, home, vehicle, or education loan, and update your KYC
details.

# Beneficiaries — What Is A Beneficiary

A beneficiary (also called a payee) is someone you have sent money to
before through this assistant, saved so you do not need to re-enter
their name and account number every time you want to pay them again.

# Beneficiaries — How They Get Saved

The first time you transfer money to someone new, you are asked for
their name and account number. Once that transfer is set up, they are
automatically saved as a beneficiary for next time — there is no
separate "add a beneficiary" step needed.

# Beneficiaries — Viewing Your Saved List

You can ask to see who you have saved, for example "list my
beneficiaries", "who do I have saved", or "show my saved payees", and
the assistant will show you the names and (masked) account numbers on
file.

# Beneficiaries — Paying Someone Already Saved

If you ask to transfer money to someone already saved, you can go
straight to entering the amount and choosing your account — you will
not be asked for their account number again.

# KYC — What Is It

KYC stands for "Know Your Customer." It is the identity and address
verification every bank must complete for its customers, and it must be
kept up to date periodically or whenever your details change.

# KYC — Documents Accepted

For a KYC update you can submit any one of the following as identity and
address proof:
- Aadhaar card
- PAN card
- Passport
- Voter ID card
- Driving licence

# KYC — When You Need To Update It

You should update your KYC details if:
- Your address has changed
- Your name has changed (e.g. after marriage)
- Your existing KYC documents have expired
- The bank has requested a periodic KYC refresh

# KYC — How Long It Takes

A KYC update is usually reviewed within 2-3 working days after the
document is submitted. You will be notified once it is approved.
